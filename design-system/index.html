Responsive layout rules

  • Full-bleed backgrounds; capped content at 1760px.
  • Fluid gutters from 20px to 120px.
  • Readable text capped near 72 characters.
  • Section rhythm scales from 72px to 144px.
  • Mobile starts at 320px without horizontal overflow.
