OUR MISSION

Purpose

MOCA Colombia strives to address the fundamental needs of small, underserved communities in Colombia and promote economic independence through agriculture. Through our agricultural initiatives, advocacies, and partnerships, we elevate standards of living and foster an environment where education, medicine, and economic empowerment create lasting change. We unlock the full potential of our farming communities and their children. We provide nutritious meals, academic support, and medical care and promote agricultural prosperity. Our efforts begin in La Argentina with MOCACAO, where we are helping to convert farmers' underperforming crops with high-yielding cacao. Steadily, we will grow to include other underserved farming communities of Colombia.

Community Challenge

La Argentina is a remote rural community where geographic isolation limits access to education, healthcare, nutritious food, and stable economic opportunities. Many families depend on small-scale agriculture, while children face barriers that can affect their health, learning, and future development. These challenges are deeply connected, requiring an integrated approach that strengthens families, supports education, and creates sustainable paths toward long-term community independence.

Long-Term Vision

Our long-term vision is a self-sustaining community where families have the resources, knowledge, and opportunities to shape their own future. By strengthening education, health, nutrition, and sustainable agriculture, we aim to create lasting progress that can continue across generations.
