OUR MISSION

MOCA Colombia strives to address the fundamental needs of small, underserved communities in Colombia and promote economic independence through agriculture. Through our agricultural initiatives, advocacies, and partnerships, we elevate standards of living and foster an environment where education, medicine, and economic empowerment create lasting change. We unlock the full potential of our farming communities and their children.

LA ARGENTINACOLOMBIA

Reaching La Argentina, home to just 273 people and isolated by miles of the Andes mountains, is an adventure. Yet, those who make the journey discover a place rich in resilience and potential. At the heart of La Argentina lies its school, a humble building that serves as the community’s center and the focus of MOCA Colombia’s efforts. The school faces immense economic hardships and a lack of resources, as access to basic necessities is limited. In La Argentina, the challenges are many, but so is the spirit of hope. With its vibrant culture centered around coffee and cacao, the community dreams of a brighter future where education, health, and prosperity are within reach.
