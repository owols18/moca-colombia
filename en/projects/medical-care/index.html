Medical Care

Access to Healthcare for Vulnerable Children

In regions like La Argentina, access to quality healthcare remains a persistent challenge, especially for children from low-income families. At MOCA Colombia, we are addressing this urgent need by partnering with Fundación Clínica Noel, a trusted pediatric institution with a century of experience serving Colombia's most vulnerable populations.

Together, we are committed to ensuring that children in La Argentina and other underserved areas receive the medical care they deserve, regardless of their economic status. By removing financial and geographic barriers, we aim to improve not just health outcomes, but the long-term well-being of entire families.

About Fundación Clínica Noel

Founded in 1924 in Medellín, Fundación Clínica Noel is a distinguished non-profit pediatric clinic recognized across Latin America for its specialized care. Each year, its dedicated interdisciplinary team provides life-changing medical services to over 50,000 children, many with craniofacial and musculoskeletal conditions.

Through our strategic partnership, MOCA Colombia and Fundación Clínica Noel are working hand in hand to:

  • Deliver specialized medical care: Including surgeries and therapies for craniofacial and musculoskeletal conditions affecting children in La Argentina.
  • Provide psychosocial support: Offering counseling and emotional support to children and families navigating complex medical diagnoses.
  • Promote preventive health education: Conducting workshops and training to raise awareness about early detection, hygiene, and available healthcare resources within the community.
